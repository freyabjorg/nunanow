MOTHERGROUND

Video Installation / Installation vidéo

Full Video Length: 10:00

2024

The video installation MOTHERGROUND immerses viewers as they walk through a vast wall covered with glacial forms, enveloping them within a pulsating red interior space.

A reversal of the figure–ground relationship takes shape through the varying patterns of costumes collaged against the environment, the pulses of strobe lighting that fragment the image, and the disorienting perspectives.
